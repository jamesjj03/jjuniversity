# JJ University Gold Style Guide

This cleaner is for style cleanup only.

- Preserve meaning and facts.
- Do not add new facts.
- Do not fact-check.
- Do not intentionally remove factual claims.
- Remove em dashes.
- Replace AI-like phrasing with natural, clean prose.
- Preserve chapter structure, paragraph intent, and HTML formatting.
- Keep paragraph tags, headings, italics, lists, links, and blockquotes intact.
- Remove bold/strong formatting unless it is part of a chapter heading, chapter title, book title, subtitle, or heading-style block.
- Preserve real quoted passages.
- Do not invent quotes, speakers, citations, or quoted wording.
- Fix broken quote/apostrophe punctuation only when it is obvious mojibake or typography cleanup.
- Keep James Johnson's direct, readable voice.
- Do not make the prose ornate, academic, poetic, corporate, or fake-professional.
- Avoid fake transitions like "in many ways."
- Avoid repeated "not only...but also" phrasing.
- Avoid repeated "it was not just" phrasing.
- Avoid robotic setup phrases unless the original genuinely needs them.
- Sentence fragments are allowed only when they already feel intentional.
- Output only the cleaned HTML/text.
