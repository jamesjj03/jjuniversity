# Scientific lineages editorial anchors

This review note strengthens the scientific lineages draft by adding chronology, text anchors, contributor placement checks, and relation logic. It is a focused editorial source brief, not a complete history of science.

## Chronology and text anchors

Mathematical astronomy and mechanics should run from Copernicus through Kepler, Galileo, and Newton. Useful anchors are Copernicus, On the Revolutions of the Heavenly Spheres; Kepler, Astronomia Nova and Harmonices Mundi; Galileo, Sidereus Nuncius and Two New Sciences; Newton, Philosophiae Naturalis Principia Mathematica and Opticks. This is an influence lineage because mathematical representation, observation, experiment, and lawlike motion accumulate across the group.

Experimental electricity and field physics should run from Franklin and Faraday into Maxwell and Hertz. Useful anchors are Franklin's Experiments and Observations on Electricity, Faraday's Experimental Researches in Electricity, Maxwell's Treatise on Electricity and Magnetism, and Hertz's Electric Waves. This lineage is not just a physics subject area; it is a handoff from experimental visualization to mathematical field theory and confirmation.

Chemistry, elements, and molecular order should run from Lavoisier through Dalton and Mendeleev to Curie. Useful anchors are Lavoisier's Elements of Chemistry, Dalton's A New System of Chemical Philosophy, Mendeleev's periodic table papers, and Curie's radioactivity work. The line moves from measurement and nomenclature into atomic theory, periodic prediction, and unstable atomic structure.

Evolutionary biology and natural history should connect Darwin, Wallace, Huxley, Fisher, Haldane, Wright, Dobzhansky, and Mayr. Useful anchors are Darwin's On the Origin of Species, the Darwin-Wallace natural selection papers, Fisher's The Genetical Theory of Natural Selection, Dobzhansky's Genetics and the Origin of Species, and Mayr's Systematics and the Origin of Species. This lineage should show selection, heredity, population thinking, and speciation.

Genetics, molecular biology, and information should connect Mendel, Morgan, Avery, Watson, Crick, Franklin, Wilkins, and McClintock. Useful anchors are Mendel's Experiments on Plant Hybridization, Morgan's The Mechanism of Mendelian Heredity, Avery-MacLeod-McCarty on DNA as transforming principle, Watson and Crick on DNA structure, Franklin's X-ray diffraction work, and McClintock on transposition.

Relativity and quantum revolutions should distinguish relativity from quantum theory while keeping them as one revolutionary physics family for a first map. Useful anchors are Einstein's 1905 relativity papers and general relativity papers, Planck's quantum hypothesis, Bohr's atomic model, Heisenberg's matrix mechanics, Schrodinger's wave mechanics, Dirac's quantum theory work, and Feynman's QED. This group needs careful objections about interpretation, measurement, and unification.

Earth systems and environmental science should connect Hutton, Lyell, Wegener, Carson, Lovelock, Margulis, and climate modeling. Useful anchors are Hutton's Theory of the Earth, Lyell's Principles of Geology, Wegener's The Origin of Continents and Oceans, Carson's Silent Spring, and Lovelock's Gaia. This lineage is about scale: deep time, planetary dynamics, ecology, atmosphere, ocean, and human consequences.

Computation, systems, and scientific modeling should connect Turing, Wiener, von Neumann, Shannon, and later simulation practice. Useful anchors are Turing's On Computable Numbers, Wiener's Cybernetics, von Neumann's work on computing architecture and automata, and Shannon's A Mathematical Theory of Communication. This group should be framed as a method lineage that changes how science represents and tests complex systems.

## Relation review

Astronomy and mechanics borrows mathematical precision into later physics and scientific modeling. Electricity and field physics reframes mechanics by treating fields as physically meaningful. Relativity and quantum theory answer failures of classical mechanics and field theory at extreme scale and microscopic scale.

Chemistry and molecular order feeds genetics and molecular biology by making matter, molecules, and measurement central. Evolutionary biology is reframed by genetics when heredity becomes population and molecular theory.

Earth systems borrows from geology, biology, chemistry, physics, and computation, but it should not be presented as a final chapter after physics. It is a cross-lineage synthesis.

Computation and systems modeling borrows from mathematics and physics while enabling modern climate, genetics, complexity, and simulation work. Its relation notes should say what modeling makes possible and what risks it introduces.

## Release checks

Do not publish generic text titles such as Source evidence for a scientist. Do not let groups become simple subject-area bins. Each group should state the handoff: what method, model, instrument, or concept was inherited and changed.
